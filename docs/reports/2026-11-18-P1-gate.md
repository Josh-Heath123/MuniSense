## What was proven

- **Exactness.** No float anywhere: floats are refused in Python and JSON. Arithmetic is exact or refused. Every rounding happens at a named policy point: the ADR-0005 points plus `pro_rating` (ADR-0011, Proposed). Credits are rounded by magnitude, so a charge and its credit cancel in every rounding mode.
- **Provenance.** Every value is a cited value. A status summary walks every field, so no value can be missed. Bills, period bills and purchases report the weakest status of every value they used, and warn about unverified values and policies.
- **The engine.** It prices forward bills (fixed, flat, block, seasonal, levies, free allocations) and splits periods across months, versions, seasons and VAT changes. Prepaid purchases use the same components and policies. A month of purchases costs what the forward engine bills for the month's kWh, within the stated roundings (`test_a_month_of_purchases_costs_what_the_month_is_billed`).
- **Ekurhuleni.** Tariff B (Residential), both supply phases, is transcribed with a citation per value. The hand-checked bills and purchases are each recalculated by the tariff-verifier from the printed values alone.

## What was NOT verified, and why

- **No Ekurhuleni value is `verified`.** The schedule is a scan, and ADR-0006 needs a human check and, for a supplier's first tariff, the owner's (E37, Q1). The checklist and the recording of `Verified` are P2/P3 work. All values are `estimated`.
- **The `R3.37,00` reading (R3.3700/kWh)** is inferred from format and magnitude. It needs an **Ekurhuleni** vending receipt or bill, which the owner does not have (E34).
- **Ekurhuleni's rounding, pro-rating and prepaid methods** are assumptions, recorded as `estimated` policies, each with a reason. They cover: cents half up; VAT once on the subtotal; factors to millionths; prepaid kWh to hundredths; the basic charge from the first purchase of the month; the monthly block reset; and sharing by days.
- **The VAT rate** is cited to a SARS guide plus Act 3 of 2026, not to the VAT Act, which SARS does not offer as a download.
- **Time-of-use, demand and export credits** are not built (P4).
